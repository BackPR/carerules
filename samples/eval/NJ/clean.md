# Saltmeadow Sparrow Child Care Center Parent Handbook

Fictional handbook written to test CareRules.

## Welcome

Welcome to Saltmeadow Sparrow Child Care Center in Gullhaven, New Jersey! We care for children from 6 weeks old until they start kindergarten. Our phone, (555) 019-4471, reaches the center directly whenever we are open, and our email is hello@saltmeadowsparrow.example.

## Our License

Saltmeadow Sparrow is licensed by the Office of Licensing in the New Jersey Department of Children and Families, and our license is posted in the front lobby. When you enroll, we give you the Office of Licensing's Information to Parents document and ask you to sign a form confirming you received it. Every staff member receives and signs for the same document.

We keep a current copy of the state's Manual of Requirements for Child Care Centers (N.J.A.C. 3A:52) at the front desk for you to read any time. To get your own copy or to learn about the licensing process, contact the Office of Licensing, Department of Children and Families, PO Box 717, Trenton, NJ 08625-0717, toll-free 1-877-667-9845.

## Hours and Closings

We are open Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., except holidays listed in our yearly calendar. If we ever plan to close temporarily or permanently, we will give you at least 30 days' notice so you have time to arrange other care.

## Enrollment

Before your child's first day, we invite you to an enrollment conference with the director. We go over our days and hours, talk about your child's habits, diet, sleep, behavior, and development, and review our policies on releasing children, toilet training, and giving medication. We also give you and discuss our communicable disease policy, the Information to Parents document, and our expulsion policy. At enrollment you will sign a form confirming that you received this handbook and each policy in it.

We also need your child's recent health examination, Universal Child Health Record, immunization record, any special care plan, your signed authorization for emergency medical treatment, and the adults you authorize to pick up your child.

Tuition is billed weekly and due each Monday; current rates are on the rate sheet in your enrollment packet. Pickup after 6:00 p.m. costs $1 per minute.

## Arrival, Pick-Up, and Release of Children

This section is our written release policy, and each parent and staff member signs to confirm receiving it.

We release your child only to you or to an adult you have authorized in writing, and new pickup adults must show photo ID. If a court order denies or limits a parent's access to a child, please give us a copy so we can keep it on file and follow it.

If no one has picked up your child by our 6:00 p.m. closing, a teacher stays with your child and keeps them supervised at all times while we call you and then each adult on your authorized list. If an hour or more has passed since closing, we still have not reached anyone who can come, and staff can no longer stay, we will call the State Central Registry Hotline at 1-877-652-2873 for help caring for your child until you or an authorized adult arrives.

If a parent or authorized adult arrives appearing physically or emotionally impaired, for example under the influence of alcohol or drugs, to the point that the director or a teacher believes your child would be at risk of harm, we will not release your child to that person. We will try to reach the child's other parent or another adult you have authorized, and if we cannot make other arrangements, we will call the State Central Registry Hotline for help caring for your child.

## Classrooms and Staffing

Teachers supervise children directly at all times and count the children in their care at every transition. Our classrooms follow New Jersey's staff-to-child ratios:

| Classroom | Age | Staff : children | Group size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minnows | Under 18 months | 1 : 4 | Groups of 4 with one primary caregiver |
| Tadpoles | 18 months to 2½ years | 1 : 6 | Groups of 6 with one primary caregiver |
| Herons | 2½ to 4 years | 1 : 10 | Up to 20 children |
| Pelicans | 4 years | 1 : 12 | Up to 20 children |
| Ospreys | 5 years | 1 : 15 | Up to 20 children |

Once every infant is asleep and every older child is resting, nap-time ratios are 1 teacher for 10 children under 18 months, 12 children from 18 months to 2½ years, or 20 children 2½ and older, with a teacher always in the nap room and enough staff in the building to return to awake ratios right away. At least two staff members go on every field trip or outing.

At least two staff members with current first aid and CPR certification are in the building whenever children are here. Staff Child Abuse Record Information (CARI) forms and related letters are kept confidential.

## Daily Activities

Our teachers prepare a written outline of each classroom's daily activities, which we keep on file and post outside each classroom. A typical preschool day:

- 7:00 a.m.: Arrival, breakfast, and quiet play
- 9:00 a.m.: Morning meeting, learning centers, and outdoor play
- 11:30 a.m.: Lunch, then rest until 2:15 p.m.
- 2:30 p.m.: Snack, music, outdoor play, and pick-up

Children here more than four hours get at least 60 minutes of active play each day, and children here less than four hours get at least 30 minutes. Weather permitting, every group goes outside daily.

## Screen Time

Children under two never use televisions, computers, tablets, or other video equipment at Saltmeadow Sparrow. For children over two, screens are used only for short educational activities that fit the child's age, never for passive viewing or in place of planned activities. Children here more than four hours may have up to 30 minutes of screen-based learning a day, and children here less than four hours may have up to 15 minutes. Teachers keep written plans on file describing each screen activity and how long it lasts, including individual plans for children with special needs.

## Meals and Nutrition

We serve breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack that meet federal Child and Adult Care Food Program standards. Every child who is with us three or more hours in a row gets a snack, and drinking water is always available. Staff never force-feed a child or pressure a child to eat.

For children under 18 months, we work out a feeding plan with you covering the feeding schedule, formula or breast milk, breastfeeding arrangements, and new foods. For babies under 12 months, the plan is written and kept where every caregiver can follow it. Bottles must be labeled with your child's name and the date, and we never warm milk or formula in a microwave.

## Rest and Safe Sleep

Babies 12 months and younger are always placed on their backs to sleep, unless your child's health care provider tells us in writing to use a different position. For safety, we never swaddle babies or other children during rest or sleep. Each baby sleeps in a crib that meets federal safety standards, with no pillows, bumper pads, or soft bedding.

Older children rest on their own labeled cot or mat, and only one child uses a crib, cot, or mat at a time. A child who has rested for 30 minutes and is not sleepy may choose a quiet activity.

## Guidance and Discipline

This section is our written discipline policy. It is posted in the front lobby, every staff member receives a copy and signs for it, and a copy is always available to you.

Our guidance is positive, fits each child's age, and helps children learn self-control. Teachers redirect, offer choices, and may offer a short calm-down break away from the group, with the child in full view of a teacher.

Children are never disciplined for not eating, not sleeping, or toileting accidents. Teachers never take away outdoor or active play time as a consequence, unless a child's behavior at that moment puts the child or others in danger. Staff never hit, shake, or use any other physical punishment, and never use abusive language, ridicule, or harsh, humiliating, or frightening treatment. We never withhold food, comfort, attention, or rest from a child.

## Expulsion Policy

A child may be expelled only if, after we have tried a written support plan with your family, the child's behavior continues to put the child or others at serious risk; if tuition is more than 30 days past due; or if a parent's behavior threatens the safety of children or staff. Before expulsion, we will share our concerns in writing, hold a parent conference, and give you at least 30 days to make other arrangements or work with us on changes that let your child stay. Immediate expulsion is possible only when a child's or parent's behavior is dangerous.

We will never expel a child because a parent complained to the Office of Licensing or questioned our policies. You will sign to confirm that you received this policy.

## Illness and Communicable Disease Policy

This section is our communicable disease policy, and you will sign to confirm you received it. A child may not attend with any of these symptoms unless a health care provider tells us in writing, or by phone with a written follow-up, that the child poses no serious health risk: severe pain; acute or bloody diarrhea; vomiting twice or more in 24 hours; a temperature of 101.5°F or higher by mouth or 100.5°F or higher under the arm, with behavior changes; unusual lethargy; yellow eyes or skin; red eyes with discharge; untreated infected skin patches; difficult or rapid breathing or severe coughing; a rash with fever or behavior changes; untreated weeping or bleeding sores; mouth sores with drooling; or a stiff neck.

If your child develops one of these symptoms here, we move your child to a separate, supervised area until you arrive or until the director speaks with a health care provider who says your child poses no serious risk. Your child may return once symptom-free or cleared by a health care provider.

A child with a disease on the New Jersey Department of Health's list of excludable communicable diseases may return only with a note from a health care provider, or after the health department tells us the child poses no risk. For chicken pox, we need your note that all sores have dried and crusted. If an excludable disease occurs at the center, every family whose child may have been exposed receives a written notice.

## Medication and Health Care Procedures

We give medication and health care procedures only after receiving your written approval, and only designated, trained staff give them. We will give prescription medication for short-term illnesses, and over-the-counter antihistamines, cough suppressants, decongestants, non-aspirin fever and pain relievers, and topical products such as sunscreen, following the label dose for your child's age and weight. We provide reasonable accommodations for children with special needs who need medication or procedures.

Prescription medication must be in its pharmacy container labeled with your child's name, the medication name and expiration date, the date prescribed, and directions. All medication is kept in a locked cabinet or out of children's reach, and we record every dose.

## Injuries and How We Notify You

We call you right away by phone if your child is bitten and the skin is broken, has any head or facial injury (including a bump on the head), falls from higher than their own height, or has an injury that needs professional medical care. For other bites and minor injuries, we send a message through our Sparrow Nest parent app and give you a written report at pickup that same day. This is our written parental notification policy, and you will sign to confirm you received it.

## Emergencies and Drills

Our written emergency procedures cover evacuation, lockdown, and natural disasters, including how we will tell you where your child is. Our alternate shelter is Gullhaven Public Library. We hold a fire drill every month, including one during nap time each year, and every child must be out of the building within three minutes. We also hold two lockdown drills each year.

## Field Trips and Transportation

We do not provide daily transportation. Before any field trip, outing, or special event that takes children away from the center by vehicle, we give you written notice at least one week ahead telling you where we are going, whether we will ride a school bus, another approved school vehicle, or a private passenger vehicle, whether the driver is a staff member, a parent, or someone else, and the name and phone number of any bus company. Your child goes only if you sign a permission slip for that trip. For walks around our neighborhood, you sign one blanket permission slip at enrollment.

On field trip buses, children ride in the car seats or boosters required for their age, at least one adult besides the driver stays with them, and children are never left unattended in a vehicle.

## Protecting Children From Abuse and Neglect

Any staff member who has reasonable cause to believe a child has been abused or neglected calls the State Central Registry Hotline at 1-877-NJ ABUSE (1-877-652-2873) immediately. Anyone may call the hotline, and reports can be made anonymously. If something unusual happens at the center that might suggest abuse or neglect involving your child, we will tell you the same day.

## Visiting and Family Involvement

You may visit the center at any time while we are open to watch your child's classroom and our program, with no need to call ahead or get permission. We also hold an annual open house, welcome volunteers, and offer conferences twice a year and on request.

## Questions, Concerns, and Complaints

You are always welcome to set a time with our director, or with our owner, who serves as the center's sponsor representative, to review and discuss any question or concern about our policies and procedures or about whether we are following the state's licensing rules. If you believe or suspect that we are violating any licensing requirement, you may report it to our director, our sponsor representative, or directly to the Office of Licensing at 1-877-667-9845.

## Social Media and Photos

We share daily photos and updates only through the private Sparrow Nest app and center email, and we post on our public Facebook page only with your written permission. Staff communicate with families only through the app, center email, and the center phone, and may not post photos of children or family information on personal social media or text families from personal phones. Parents may not post photos or videos of any child other than their own taken at the center or at center events. Every parent and staff member receives a copy of this social media policy and signs to confirm receiving it.

## Records and Privacy

Your child's records are confidential and are shared only with you, with authorized state agencies, or with your written permission. We keep children's records for one year after a child leaves.

Thank you for joining the Saltmeadow Sparrow family!
