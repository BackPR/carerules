# Thimbleberry Hollow Early Learning Center: Family Handbook

Fictional handbook written to test CareRules.

## Welcome

Welcome to Thimbleberry Hollow! We are a certified child care center for children from 6 weeks through 5 years old. Every family receives a copy of this handbook at enrollment and whenever a policy changes.

- **Director:** Renata Okafor-Lindqvist, responsible for daily operation
- **Business address:** 2250 NE Wren Hollow Road, Salem, OR 97301
- **Business phone:** (503) 555-0148

## Our Philosophy

We believe young children learn through play, relationships, and curiosity. They grow when they choose meaningful activities, try things out, and talk about their discoveries with caring adults. Teachers put this into practice by planning each week around the children's interests, setting up centers for art, blocks, books, and science, and joining children's play to add new ideas.

## Center Description, Hours, and Closures

Thimbleberry Hollow is certified for 60 children. We currently care for 58 children: 8 infants, 10 toddlers, and 40 preschoolers. We are open Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., all twelve months of the year.

We close for New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the following Friday, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day, plus two staff training days posted at least 30 days ahead. We do not provide evening or night care.

## Classrooms and Staffing

We follow Oregon's staff-to-child ratios and group sizes at all times:

| Age of children | Classroom | Staff to children | Largest group |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 weeks to 24 months | Sprout Room | 1 to 4 | 8 |
| 24 to 36 months | Fern Room | 1 to 5 | 10 |
| 36 months to school age | Cedar and Alder Rooms | 1 to 10 | 20 each |

In the Fern Room, two teachers care for no more than 10 toddlers, which keeps us at one teacher for every five children. Teachers supervise children within sight and sound and never rely on baby monitors or cameras in place of a teacher in the room. Each classroom has at least 35 square feet of activity space per child, and our playground has more than 75 square feet for each child using it at one time.

## Enrollment and What Families Provide

Before your child's first day, please complete the enrollment form, emergency contacts and authorized pickup list, health history, emergency medical authorization, and your child's Oregon Certificate of Immunization Status.

You are responsible for keeping this information current. Please tell us right away about new phone numbers, addresses, pickup persons, allergies, medications, or health changes, and review and sign the enrollment form at least once a year. Please provide labeled spare clothes, outdoor clothing, a small blanket for rest, and for infants and toddlers, diapers, wipes, and formula or breast milk.

When we learn that a child with specific needs has applied or is enrolled, we complete an individualized assessment with the family to identify reasonable changes to our space, policies, and practices.

## Tuition and Fees

Tuition is billed monthly and due on the 1st; a $20 late fee applies after the 5th. The registration fee is $75. Please give two weeks' written notice before withdrawing.

## Arrival and Departure

Please walk your child into the classroom and stay until a teacher has greeted and accepted your child. Sign your child in and out each day on the tablet at the classroom door.

We release children only to parents and to adults you have named on your child's authorized pickup list. Anyone we don't know must show photo identification. If someone not on the list needs to pick up, call the director ahead of time; we record the date and time, how long the permission lasts, who gave it, who may pick up, and which staff member took the call.

## A Day at Thimbleberry Hollow

| Time | Preschool day |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | Arrival and free choice centers |

| 9:15 | Small groups and learning centers |
| 10:00 | Snack, then outdoor play |

Every day includes at least an hour of active physical play, indoors if the weather is unsafe. Infants and toddlers follow their own feeding and sleeping rhythms. We don't use screens with children under 2. Older children may occasionally watch a short video tied to a project, never more than 2.5 hours in a week, with another activity always available.

## Holidays and Celebrations

We do not hold religious activities. We celebrate our families' cultures by inviting them to share songs, foods, stories, and traditions, and we recognize holidays through books and conversations about how different families celebrate. We ask your permission before your child joins a cultural event or a celebration where food is served.

## Rest and Safe Sleep

Toddlers and preschoolers rest after lunch on their own labeled cot. Children who have not fallen asleep after 30 minutes may get up for quiet books and puzzles.

Infants are always placed on their backs to sleep, on the firm, flat mattress of their own crib. Babies who roll over on their own may stay in the position they choose. Cribs hold only a fitted sheet and, if you wish, a plain pacifier: no blankets, bumpers, toys, bibs, or hats, and we never swaddle. If a baby arrives asleep in a car seat or dozes off elsewhere, we move them right away to their crib. Teachers stay in the room and check sleeping infants often for breathing and warmth.

## Guidance and Discipline

We use positive guidance to help children learn self-control and respect for others: clear expectations, redirection, problem-solving together, and praise for kind choices.

We never hit, spank, shake, or handle a child roughly; never isolate a child in a closed or dark space; never withhold or force food, rest, or toileting; never yell harshly, shame, or tease; and never punish toileting accidents, even if a parent asks us to.

If a child's behavior could hurt themselves or others, a teacher moves other children away, stays calm and close, and uses words and comfort to help the child settle. Physical restraint is used only when someone's safety is threatened, and only as a gentle hold for the shortest time needed. We tell you the same day, document it, and notify the Child Care Licensing Division (CCLD).

## Keeping Every Child Enrolled

Our goal is for every child to stay with us. When a teacher has ongoing concerns about a child's behavior, or right after any unsafe incident, the teacher asks the director for support. The director responds within one business day by observing, adjusting routines or staffing, and coaching the teacher, and may add visual schedules or a support plan.

If those steps aren't enough, we request help from Every Child Belongs, Oregon's free program that helps centers support young children, and at the same time invite you to an in-person conference to plan together. We document the behaviors, triggers, and strategies tried. A temporary safety-based break from care is used only for serious safety threats and ends as soon as supports are in place.

## Meals, Snacks, and Allergies

We serve breakfast, lunch, and three snacks that meet CACFP guidelines, so children never go more than 3½ hours without a meal or snack. The weekly menu is posted at the entrance. Teachers sit with children, encourage new foods without forcing them, offer second helpings, and never use food as a reward or punishment. We follow written special diets for medical, religious, or cultural needs. Water is always available. Food is stored covered, labeled, and at safe temperatures, away from cleaning products.

Food from home must be ready to eat and labeled with your child's name; we supplement it if needed.

For any child with an allergy, we write a care plan with you listing the allergen, signs of a reaction, and treatment steps, and train every teacher and our cook on it. If your child is exposed to an allergen, we call you immediately; if epinephrine is given, we call 911.

## Infant Care

Each infant has a written feeding plan and is fed when hungry. Nursing parents are always welcome to feed their babies here, and the Sprout Room has a quiet glider for you. Bottles must be labeled with your baby's full name and the date. We refrigerate them, warm them in a bottle warmer (never a microwave), hold babies during feedings, never prop bottles, and return unused breast milk at pickup. Pacifiers are labeled, stored separately in a closed container, and sanitized daily.

## Health and Hygiene

Children and staff wash hands with soap and running water on arrival, before and after eating, after toileting or diapering, after outdoor play, and after touching animals. We check diapers at least every 2 hours and change them promptly, following the procedure posted at each changing table. Toilet learning begins when your child shows interest, and we plan it with you so it stays relaxed and pressure-free. Each child's bedding is labeled and laundered weekly, or sooner if soiled; crib sheets are changed weekly and whenever soiled.

We track every child's immunizations as Oregon Health Authority rules require and remind you when an update is due.

**Sunscreen and insect repellent.** With your written permission, renewed yearly, we apply sunscreen before outdoor play and insect repellent when needed, following the label. Products you send must have your child's name on them; if we supply sunscreen, we tell you the brand.

## When Your Child Is Ill

Children must stay home, or be picked up, with a fever over 100.4°F (return after 24 hours fever-free without medicine); diarrhea (return 48 hours after it stops); unexplained vomiting (return 48 hours after the last episode); severe or persistent cough; yellow skin or eyes; draining sores that can't be covered; stiff neck with headache; difficulty breathing; severe pain; pus-filled eyes; or unusual tiredness that keeps them from joining in. A child with a restrictable disease may return only with written approval from a health care provider or public health.

If your child becomes ill here, we separate them where a teacher can see and hear them, give them a cot, and call you to pick up as soon as possible.

## Medication

Before we give any medicine, prescription or over-the-counter, you must complete and sign a dated medication authorization form for that medicine. Medicine must be in its original container with your child's name on it, and prescriptions must carry the pharmacy label. For a single dose of a non-prescription medicine, we may take your permission by phone; we record the date and time, and you sign at pickup. For chronic conditions such as asthma, one authorization may cover up to 12 months. We record every dose, tell you about it daily, and store medicines locked away from children and food.

## Injuries and Emergencies

Minor scrapes are cleaned and bandaged, and we tell you about them the same day. We call you immediately for any bump to the head or any injury that may need a doctor, and we call 911 for serious emergencies. Our emergency hospital is Willamette Ridge Medical Center. For serious injuries, we complete an incident report and ask you to sign it within 48 hours.

Our written emergency plan covers fire, earthquake, severe weather, power outages, a lost child, a hostile intruder, and medical emergencies. We evacuate to the church parking lot next door, more than 50 feet from our building, and if we must leave the area we relocate to Maple Bend Grange Hall, 1.2 miles away. For shelter-in-place or lockdown, children move to interior rooms. Teachers carry attendance sheets, emergency contacts, and medical authorizations. When an emergency ends, we notify you by text and reunite children only with authorized adults.

We practice a fire evacuation drill every month, and every other month we also practice another part of our plan, such as an earthquake drill, shelter-in-place, or lockdown.

## Safety Practices

Teachers check the playground every morning for hazards. Cleaning products and other toxic materials are locked away from children. Pest control products are applied only when children are not present, and children return only when the label says it is safe.

Tobacco, vaping devices, cannabis, alcohol, and illegal drugs are not allowed on our property, within 10 feet of our doors and windows, in vehicles, or on field trips. Weapons are never allowed.

The Cedar Room has two guinea pigs in a covered cage. Children may help feed them with a teacher beside them.

Children enjoy water tables and summer sprinklers with a teacher close by. We do not offer swimming; if we ever did, we would first get your written permission and share our safety plan.

## Visitors

Visitors such as repair workers, vendors, and student observers sign our visitor log with their name, reason for visiting, and times in and out. Any adult who is not enrolled in the Central Background Registry is always accompanied by a staff member and is never alone with children.

## Field Trips and Transportation

Field trip notices showing the date, times, and destination are posted at least 48 hours ahead. Before each trip, we ask you to sign a permission slip listing that trip's date, times, and location.

For trips that need driving, we use our center van. Drivers are staff at least 21 years old with a valid license, a clean driving record, and first aid and CPR training. Our van is insured, maintained, and fitted with properly installed car seats. Ratios are kept in the van, attendance is taken whenever children get on or off, and children are never left unattended inside or outside a vehicle.

## Abuse and Neglect

Staff are trained to recognize signs of abuse and on Oregon's reporting laws, and we teach children simple body-safety words.

Every staff member is a mandatory reporter and must report any suspicion of abuse or neglect immediately to the Oregon Child Abuse Hotline (1-855-503-7233), DHS Child Welfare, or law enforcement.

## Communication and Concerns

We share news through our family app, a monthly newsletter, and daily written reports for infants and toddlers. Conferences are held twice a year, or anytime you ask. If you have a concern, please talk first with your child's teacher; if it isn't resolved, contact the director, who will respond in writing within five business days.

Custodial parents may visit all child care areas at any time during our hours, without advance notice. Just let any staff member know you are here.

## Records and Privacy

Your child's records are confidential and available only to staff who need them, to you as your child's parent, and to CCLD staff.

## Licensing Information

Our most recent CCLD inspection report and the Oregon rules for certified child care centers are available on request at the front desk. Visit the Department of Early Learning and Care at www.oregonearlylearning.com or call 1-800-556-6616 to read the rules, reach CCLD, or report a complaint. The Oregon Child Abuse Hotline is 1-855-503-7233.

You can also look up information about any child care provider, including us, on Oregon's child care safety portal.

Thank you for trusting us with your child!
