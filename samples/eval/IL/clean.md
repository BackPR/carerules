# Prairie Lantern Early Learning Center: Parent Handbook

Fictional handbook written to test CareRules.

## Welcome

Welcome to Prairie Lantern Early Learning Center, a licensed day care center in Willow Ford, Illinois, serving children from 6 weeks through 12 years old. Our purpose is to give every child a safe, warm place to play, learn, and grow, and our goals are to build confidence, curiosity, and kindness while supporting working families.

This handbook is our written statement of purposes and policies. We give it to every family at enrollment, and anyone may ask for a copy at the front office.

## Who Is Responsible

Prairie Lantern is operated by Prairie Lantern Learning, Inc., whose board of directors is legally responsible for the program (412 Marigold Lane, Willow Ford, IL; 555-014-7720). Our director, Dana Whitfield, runs daily operations at the same address and phone number.

The board has filed with the Illinois Department of Children and Family Services (DCFS) a signed written statement that delegates day-to-day responsibility for licensing compliance to the director and describes the lines of communication among the board, staff, and parents. Parents bring questions to the teacher or director, who reports to the board.

We notify DCFS immediately of any major change affecting our operation, such as a change in governance, location, finances, or key staff, or a fire. We notify DCFS in writing within 7 days after receiving notice of any legal action against the center that could affect our operation. Authorized DCFS representatives are admitted at any time during our hours of operation.

We carry public liability insurance of $500,000 per occurrence, and every enrolled child is covered by our student accident insurance.

## Our Staff

Our written staffing plan keeps the same primary teachers with each group every day, for continuity of care and supervision. During orientation, the director and every new staff member review the Child Care Act of 1969, the Abused and Neglected Child Reporting Act, and the licensing standards that apply to their jobs, and the review date is recorded in each personnel file. A complete, current copy of the licensing standards is kept in the staff room, and our written personnel policies are available to staff at all times.

Our written risk management plan identifies risks in our operation, explains how we reduce them, and sets procedures for emergencies and crises. It covers staff training in universal precautions, building and vehicle upkeep, food sanitation, severe weather alerts, and fire, tornado, and disaster plans. It also includes our staff vaccination policy, which follows CDC guidelines: staff show proof of Tdap and MMR vaccination or immunity.

## Enrollment

We enroll children under our written enrollment policy. We never use eligibility criteria that screen out children with disabilities, and we make reasonable changes to our practices so children with disabilities can take part. Before you enroll, we give you information about our program and invite you to observe a classroom at any time during our hours.

At enrollment you receive this handbook, the DCFS summary of licensing standards, and other DCFS materials, and you sign a statement, kept in your child's record, that you received them.

**Proof of identity notice:** Within 30 days of enrollment, you must give us a certified copy of your child's birth certificate or other reliable proof of identity and age. If proof is not provided within 30 days, the law requires us to notify the Illinois State Police or local law enforcement.

**Health records:** Before your child starts, we need a medical report on the DCFS form showing the immunizations required by the Illinois Department of Public Health. For children not yet in school, it must be dated within 6 months before enrollment, and it is valid for 2 years. You may ask that immunizations, physical exams, or medical treatment be waived on religious grounds; the request must be in writing, signed by you, and is kept in your child's record.

## Hours and Holidays

We are open Monday through Friday, 6:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and we close on major holidays listed on our yearly calendar. A child may not stay more than 12 hours in any 24-hour period unless your work or training schedule requires it and you confirm this in writing.

## Tuition and Fees

A non-refundable $100 registration fee is due at enrollment. Weekly tuition ranges from $160 for school-age children to $340 for infants, paid in advance weekly or monthly by automatic withdrawal. A $20 late fee is added when payment is more than three days late. If tuition is two weeks overdue, the director will meet with you to set up a payment plan, and if an account is 30 days overdue without a plan, we may end care with two weeks' written notice.

## Arrival and Departure

Families drop off and pick up children at the classroom; we provide transportation only for school-age children between the center and Willow Ford Elementary. Please sign your child in on the attendance log. A teacher checks each child for obvious signs of illness before the child joins the group.

We release children only to parents and to adults on the primary or contingency pickup list. Anyone not known to our staff must show a driver's license or other photo ID. When a child goes home with someone on the contingency list, we record that person's name and the date and time. The time of every departure is written on the daily departure log and initialed by the person picking up.

A school-age child may leave the center alone only under an individual plan with your written authorization stating the release time, how the child will travel and when they will return, what we will do if the child does not return on time, and which staff member will log the departure.

## Late Pickup

At enrollment you will sign our late pickup agreement. A late fee of $1 per minute begins at 6:01 p.m. At 6:10 p.m. we begin calling you and then each emergency contact, and we keep calling every 10 minutes, so please keep your phone numbers up to date. If we cannot reach anyone by 7:00 p.m., we will contact the Willow Ford Police and, if needed, the DCFS hotline. Two staff members stay with your child and remain responsible for your child's safety until you or the authorities arrive. Staff never blame your child, and the situation is discussed only with you.

## Our Daily Program

Each classroom's daily program is posted by its door. It sets regular times for meals, rest, and toileting, with flexibility for each child, and balances active and quiet play, indoors and outdoors, every day.

## Meals and Snacks

Children are offered a meal or snack at least every three hours, and no closer than two hours apart, unless they are asleep. Menus are posted outside the kitchen and each classroom, and copies are available on request. A child who needs a special diet for medical, allergy, or religious reasons receives meals and snacks that follow written instructions from you, your clergy, or your child's medical provider.

## Rest and Safe Sleep

Children under 6 who are not yet in kindergarten and stay 5 hours or more have a rest time on their own labeled cot.

Infants sleep in their own crib with a firm, tight-fitting mattress. Infants are always placed on their backs to sleep. Babies who can easily roll both ways may then settle into their own position. A different sleep position is used only with written instructions signed by your child's physician. No infant is put to sleep on a sofa, in a car seat, or in a swing. Soft bedding, bumpers, pillows, quilts, comforters, sheepskins, and stuffed toys are never used or stored in cribs.

## Guidance and Discipline

We believe children learn self-control through caring relationships and clear limits. Teachers explain limits before any correction and use logical consequences in proportion to the behavior. With infants and toddlers, we use firm positive statements and redirection. Children help create their classroom rules and get chances to solve their own conflicts. We share concerns with you early and plan with you. Staff sign this policy when hired, and parents sign it at enrollment.

Our written rules for children are posted with pictures in every classroom: We use gentle hands and feet. We use walking feet inside. We use kind words. We take care of our things.

A child 2 or older who needs help calming down may take a short break away from the group, never longer than one minute per year of age, and children under 24 months are never removed from the group. We never use corporal punishment, including hitting, spanking, swatting, shaking, or pinching. We never threaten or withhold food, rest, or bathroom use, use abusive or profane language, or humiliate, shame, reject, terrorize, or isolate a child. We never use exercise or missed active play as a punishment, and children are never disciplined for toilet accidents.

### Behavior Support and Program Transitions

If a child shows a pattern of challenging behavior, we write a behavior support plan with you and, with your consent, involve qualified professionals such as Early Intervention. Any clinical behavior management plan is developed with you and a professional clinician and documented in your child's file. We do not expel children because of challenging behavior.

If, after documented attempts to meet their needs, an infant, toddler, or preschooler cannot benefit from our program or their presence is harmful to the group, we will plan with you to find a better-suited program and work with you and that program on a transition plan that keeps care continuous. We notify DCFS of every transition plan.

### Withdrawing Your Child

You may withdraw your child at any time. We will ask you for a written statement of your reason and keep it on file. If you cannot provide a letter, the director records your name, your relationship to the child, and the withdrawal date, and signs and dates that record.

## Health and Illness

Children with diarrhea, or with a rash together with a fever (101°F or higher by mouth, or 100°F or higher under the arm), may not attend while those symptoms last and will be sent home if the symptoms start during the day. We also exclude children with fever and behavior change, vomiting two or more times in 24 hours, difficulty breathing, head lice until the morning after the first treatment, or any reportable disease that requires isolation.

## Medication

This is our written medication policy. We accept prescription and over-the-counter medicine only in its original container. Prescription medicine must have the full pharmacy label and is given only as the physician directs, with your signed release on file and updated regularly. Over-the-counter medicine is given according to the manufacturer's instructions only when you provide it with written permission. A designated staff member records the date, time, dosage, prescription number, and their name for each dose. Diaper ointment, sunscreen, and insect repellent are used only after you approve them in writing.

## Accidents and Emergencies

If a child needs a doctor or hospital, we call 911 or arrange care at Willow Ford Community Hospital, using the emergency treatment consent you sign at enrollment. We notify you immediately of any illness, accident, or injury.

Any accident or injury that needs professional medical care, or any other emergency, is entered in your child's record and reported by phone immediately to you and to our DCFS licensing office. We hold fire drills monthly and tornado drills twice a year.

## Field Trips and Transportation

Preschool and school-age classes take walking trips to Linden Park and the library and occasional museum trips in our center minibus. We post each trip, with its date and transportation, at least one week ahead, and your child goes only with your signed permission.

Our drivers meet Illinois requirements for age, license, medical exam, and driving record. Staff-to-child ratios are kept on every ride, and each child rides in a federally approved car seat, booster, or seat belt suited to their age. The driver carries a written emergency plan for accidents, serious illness, and severe weather alerts. Our written plan for scheduled school transportation lists the route schedule, the people authorized to receive each child, what we do if no authorized adult is present, and our safety precautions.

## Personal Belongings

Please label everything your child brings, including a full change of clothes for their cubby. Toddlers and preschoolers may bring one small blanket or soft toy for their cot. Please keep other toys, money, candy, and electronics at home, except on Friday sharing days. We cannot be responsible for lost or damaged items.

## Communicating With Families

We stay in touch through a daily report on our parent app, a monthly newsletter, the parent bulletin board, and conferences twice a year.

Our written plan for parent visits encourages you to observe and take part in your child's day. You may visit without an appointment any time we are open. We invite you to read a story, share a family tradition, or join a field trip.

## Confidentiality and Records

Information about your child's admission, progress, health, or discharge is confidential and shared only with staff the director designates and DCFS representatives, unless you give written permission. Before we release information to anyone else, you sign a release form naming who may receive it and how long the release is valid. We never share personal information about your child or family without your written permission. Children's records are kept for 5 years after a child leaves our program, general and financial records for 5 years, and daily attendance records by group for one year.

## Lead Testing of Our Drinking Water

Because our building was built in 1978 and we serve children under six, our water is tested for lead by a certified laboratory. All results, including those below 2.01 ppb, are posted on the parent bulletin board beside the front office. One classroom sink tested above 2.01 ppb, so it now has a certified lead-removing filter, and our mitigation plan is posted with the results.

## Reporting Child Abuse and Neglect

Every Prairie Lantern staff member is a mandated reporter. Staff who suspect a child has been abused or neglected must report it immediately to the DCFS Child Abuse/Neglect Hotline at 1-800-252-2873.
