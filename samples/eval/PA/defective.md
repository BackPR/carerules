# Thistledown Hollow Learning Center Parent Handbook

Fictional handbook written to test CareRules.

## Welcome to Thistledown Hollow

Welcome! We are so glad your family has chosen Thistledown Hollow Learning Center in Wrenfield, Pennsylvania. Our goal is simple: to give every child a safe, warm, and playful place to grow, and to give every parent peace of mind. We care for children from 6 weeks old until they start kindergarten.

Please keep this handbook handy and ask us anything. You can reach the front desk at (555) 014-2290 or hello@thistledownhollow.example.



## Hours and Closings

We are open Monday through Friday from 6:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. We do not provide night care. We close on major holidays listed in our yearly calendar. If snow or an emergency forces us to close or open late, we will send a text and app message by 5:30 a.m.

## Enrollment

Before your child starts, the director meets with you to review our general daily schedule, hours of care, fees, who provides meals, clothing needs, health policies, supervision policies, night care policy, dismissal policies, and transportation and pick-up arrangements. This handbook gives you that same information in writing, and you will receive your own copy when you enroll. We also invite your child to visit before the first day.

Each family signs an enrollment agreement with the director. The agreement lists your weekly fee and the day it is due, the services we provide to your family and child, your child's usual arrival and departure times, the adults you authorize to pick up your child, your child's admission date, and any services that cost extra. You keep the original signed agreement and we keep a copy. The agreement also includes the Department's form for sharing information about your child's growth and development, which we review and update with you every six months.

Emergency contact information is required for every child before the first day, including your phone numbers and addresses, your child's doctor, health insurance, special needs, and your signed consent for emergency medical care. Please update it in writing at least once every six months, and right away whenever anything changes.

## Fees and Payment

Tuition is charged by the week and is due each Monday. Weekly rates range from $265 for preschoolers to $335 for infants, plus a one-time $75 registration fee. Extra services, such as field trip tickets, are listed in your agreement and billed separately. Pickup after 6:00 p.m. costs $1 per minute. To withdraw, please give us two weeks' written notice.

## Dismissal From Care

We may end care if tuition is more than two weeks past due, or if, after working on it together, we cannot safely meet your child's needs. We will give you two weeks' written notice, except when there is an immediate safety concern. We also follow the Pennsylvania Department of Health's immunization rules for child care (28 Pa. Code § 27.77), which may require us to exclude a child whose immunization records are not up to date.

## Arrival and Pick-Up

Please walk your child into the classroom and sign in on the tablet by the door. Use the marked drop-off lane; our safe walking and parking routes are posted in the lobby.

We release your child only to you or to an adult you have named in writing on your enrollment agreement. Either parent may pick up unless a court order on file with us says otherwise. We will check photo ID the first few times someone new picks up.

In a true emergency, you may call and give us the name of someone who is not on your list. We will check that person's photo ID before releasing your child, and we will record in your child's file your name, the date and time of your call, the name of the person picking up, and the names of the staff members who took the call and released your child.

## Classrooms and Staffing

Every classroom follows Pennsylvania's staff-to-child ratios and group sizes:

| Classroom | Age | Staff : children | Maximum group size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprouts | Infants, 6 weeks to 1 year | 1 : 4 | 8 |
| Seedlings | Young toddlers, 1 to 2 years | 1 : 5 | 10 |
| Saplings | Older toddlers, 2 to 3 years | 1 : 6 | 12 |
| Oaks | Preschool, 3 years to kindergarten | 1 : 10 | 20 |

Every child is assigned to a specific teacher who knows where each child in the group is at all times, on our grounds and on outings. When we combine age groups early in the morning or late in the day, we staff the room at the preschool ratio of one teacher to ten children, even when a few toddlers join the group. At least two adults, at least one of them a staff person, are in the building whenever two or more children are here, and at least two go on every outing.

While toddlers and preschoolers nap, we keep at least one teacher for every 10 young toddlers, 12 older toddlers, or 20 preschoolers, and teachers on duty stay in the classroom area during rest time.

## Our Daily Schedule

Each classroom has a written plan of daily activities and routines, including time for free play, posted on the classroom door. A typical preschool day looks like this:

- 6:30 to 8:00 a.m.: Arrival, quiet centers, and breakfast
- 8:30 a.m.: Morning circle, then learning centers and free play
- 10:15 a.m.: Outdoor play
- 11:30 a.m.: Lunch
- 12:15 to 2:15 p.m.: Rest time
- 2:30 p.m.: Afternoon snack, then art, music, and outdoor play
- 5:00 to 6:00 p.m.: Stories, free play, and pick-up

Weather permitting, every group goes outdoors every day. Infants follow their own feeding and sleeping schedules, and our teachers spend the day holding, rocking, talking with, and playing with them.

## Rest Time and Safe Sleep

Toddlers and preschoolers rest on their own cot, labeled with their name and used only by them. We send cot sheets and blankets home every Friday for washing.

Each infant has an assigned crib labeled with the baby's name. We never use stacked cribs, and our crib slats are no more than 2 3/8 inches apart. Babies usually sleep on their backs in their own crib, but if your baby sleeps better on the tummy or side at home, just let us know and we will follow your preference. Toys, bumper pads, and pillows are never in a crib with a sleeping baby, and no baby or toddler is put down to sleep with a bottle.

## Guidance and Discipline

We teach children to get along by modeling kind words, offering choices, and redirecting. When a child needs a break, a teacher stays nearby and helps the child calm down and rejoin the group.

Our staff never spank or use any other physical punishment. We never ridicule a child, threaten a child or family, or use harsh, demeaning, or abusive language. We never restrain a child with ties or straps, and we never shut a child in a closet, locked room, or other confined space.

## Meals, Snacks, and Allergies

Children who are with us four or more hours receive nutritious meals and snacks at regular times: breakfast, a hot lunch, and an afternoon snack. Lunch always includes a dairy food, a protein, a fruit or vegetable, and a grain, and breakfast includes foods from at least three of those four groups. Portions are sized for young children, and seconds are available whenever a child is still hungry.



Food is never withheld as a punishment, and we never force a child to eat. Please tell us about any allergy at enrollment. If your child needs a special diet, we follow the written instructions and consent you give us.

For infants, please give us a written statement of your baby's formula or breast milk and feeding schedule. We introduce new foods only after talking with you. Bottles must be labeled with your baby's name. Babies 6 months and younger are always held during bottle feedings, and we never warm bottles in a microwave.

## Health Records and Immunizations

Please give us your child's health report, signed by a physician, physician assistant, or CRNP, no later than 60 days after your child's first day. Updated reports are due every 6 months for infants and young toddlers and every 12 months for older toddlers and preschoolers.

Children 2 months and older may not stay at the center more than 60 days after their first day unless you give us written verification of the dates of each immunization from a physician, physician assistant, CRNP, or health department. If your child is exempt from immunizations for religious or strong personal beliefs, we need a written, signed, and dated statement from you. A medical exemption requires a written, signed, and dated statement from your child's physician, physician assistant, or CRNP.

## When Your Child Is Sick

If your child becomes ill at the center, we will call you as soon as possible and keep your child comfortable and apart from the group until you arrive.

If your child shows symptoms of a contagious illness that could threaten the health of other children, we will ask you to pick up, and your child may return once we receive a note from a physician or CRNP saying your child is no longer a threat to the health of others. We will let all families know when there is a suspected outbreak of a contagious disease at the center.

## Medication

Our teachers can give prescription and over-the-counter medicine whenever you ask; a quick phone call or a word at drop-off telling us the dose is all we need. Medicine must come in its original container, labeled with the medicine's name and your child's name, and prescription medicine also needs the prescriber's instructions (the pharmacy label is fine). We store all medicine in a locked cabinet or out of children's reach, and we record every dose in a medication log with the date, time, amount, and the initials of the teacher who gave it.

## Injuries and Emergencies

A first-aid kit is kept in every classroom, out of children's reach. If your child has a minor injury, we will give first aid and give you a written report the same day.

If your child needs emergency room treatment or is admitted to a hospital, we will notify you immediately, and a staff member will go with your child and stay until you arrive. We also report the event to the Department of Human Services by phone within 24 hours and in writing within 72 hours. We make the same reports if a child is ever lost or missing, left behind on an outing, or left at the center after closing, and if a fire at the center requires the fire department.



We hold a fire drill twice a year, in the fall and in the spring, at different times of day and during different activities, and we change the pretend location of the fire each time. Evacuation routes are posted on every floor. The emergency plan is posted in the front hallway, reviewed at least once a year, and practiced in an emergency drill every year.

Weapons, firearms, and ammunition are not permitted anywhere in the center.

## Field Trips and Transportation

Thistledown Hollow does not provide daily transportation. A few times a year, our preschoolers take field trips on a chartered bus driven by a licensed driver who is at least 18 years old. We never transport a child without your written permission, so we send home a permission slip for each trip, and we ask for your signed permission for neighborhood walks at enrollment.

On every trip, children ride in the car seat or booster seat Pennsylvania law requires, our ratios apply without counting the driver, a first-aid kit and emergency contact information come along, and children are never left alone in a vehicle.

## Protecting Children From Abuse

Every member of our staff is a mandated reporter under Pennsylvania law. Any staff member who suspects that a child has been abused brings the concern to the director, who reviews the situation and decides whether a report to ChildLine is needed. Within 48 hours, the director or a designated staff person also sends a written report to the county children and youth agency.

## Records and Privacy

Your child's records are confidential and kept in a locked cabinet. We do not share information about your child or family without your written consent, except with agents of the Department of Human Services during inspections and investigations. You may see your child's complete record at any time, and we keep a copy of your child's record for at least one year after your child leaves, unless you ask us to transfer the whole record to you or to another program.

## Families Are Always Welcome

You are welcome to visit any part of the center whenever your child is here, without calling ahead. The only exception is when a court order limiting a parent's access is on file with us.

We love having families involved, from reading to a class to our spring picnic and fall and spring conferences. We announce these opportunities on the parent board and by email, and we keep a yearly file of those announcements. If you prefer information in a language other than English, please tell us.

## Questions and Concerns

If something is not working for your family, please talk with your child's teacher first. The director is also available every weekday and will meet with you within two business days.

## Clothing and Belongings

Please send a labeled change of clothes and outdoor gear for the season. Families of children in diapers provide disposable diapers and wipes, and teachers check diapers at least every two hours and change them whenever they are wet or soiled.

Thank you for trusting us with your child. We look forward to growing together!
